Impact

Our vision is to transform underused roadside spaces into resilient, biodiverse corridors that support pollinators, birds, and local ecosystems while reducing maintenance needs and improving climate resilience.

Goals for the next year

  • Add 500 plants across 5 sites locally.
  • Support new chapters by sharing guidance and resources to replicate projects in their communities.

We are just getting started — donations help us grow our work and reach. Thank you for supporting Terravia.
